How to get paid

  1. Sign up on the ThundrrGO app so your posts get tracked and paid.
  2. Film at a hotel from your list. Public areas are fine — you do not need to book a room.
  3. Tag the hotel's location in the post. If the location isn't tagged, it doesn't count.
  4. The post has to be valid, at least 15 seconds long, and reach 300 views to qualify.
  5. You are paid per hotel, not per video. One qualifying video at a P1 hotel earns $10; one at a P0 hotel earns $5. You can claim 4 P1 hotels ($40) and 4 P0 hotels ($20) — $60 maximum, from 8 videos at 8 different hotels.
  6. More videos at the same hotel do not pay more here — but they all still count toward the 20-video Posting Challenge, so nothing is wasted. To earn more on this campaign, film a different hotel.
  7. Travelling is worth it. Your list includes every county within about a two hour drive. Eight different hotels — four P1 and four P0 — is the full $60, and that is doable in a day or two of driving.
